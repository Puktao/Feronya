Our Story

A warm welcome in Taksim since 1996

Since 1996, Feronya Hotel has welcomed guests to the heart of Istanbul with traditional Turkish hospitality and a contemporary approach to service, centred on guest satisfaction.

Our four-star city hotel has 102 rooms, including 88 standard rooms and 14 junior suites, a comfortable base for both business and leisure stays.

From Taksim, Istanbul's many layers are close at hand: the Golden Horn, Topkapı, Galata and Hagia Sophia, along with the city's shopping streets, culture and nightlife, and its business and exhibition centres.

Feel, discover and experience Istanbul with us.
