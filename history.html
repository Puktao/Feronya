Our History

1968 — PRESENT

From Maritime Union Headquarters to Boutique Icon

Originally constructed in the late 1960s with its unmistakable white-tiled facade and signature circular porthole windows, the building celebrated New York's deep seafaring traditions.

Reimagined as a luxury boutique hotel, every detail pays homage to mid-century ocean liners—from warm teak paneling and brass hardware to serene garden terraces overlooking Ninth Avenue.
